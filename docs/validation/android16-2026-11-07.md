- Captured Settings and the demo in light and dark mode. Waited for stable screenshots, inspected their pixels, and compared actual DOM paint and dimensions through WebView debugging.

| Toggle | State icon in native Settings | Optional helper renders the checked icon; native Ionic on/off labels suppress that helper icon |

Corner estimates fit the flat-color boundary of the raster image. Antialiasing and pixel snapping introduce approximately one physical pixel of uncertainty; these are measurements, not exact equality claims. Alert layout retains Ionic sizing and differs from the native Settings dialog in width and content height. Its outline rasterizes to two physical pixels on this device despite a 1 CSS px declaration.

Additional checks in both modes: mixed `mode="ios"` title/subtitle retain their reference colors; individual tab icon opt-out inherits the parent color; an explicit icon color wins; Range's gap follows the painted parent even when its own `color` differs; multiline list text no longer overlaps; accordion gaps are 2 / 2 / 0 px.

## Automated and visual review boundaries

Linux Chromium passed the 112 non-alignment cases on Ionic 9, including 80 route screenshots. The new four-case Alert alignment suite initially caught unstable transition measurements; after waiting for presentation and stable layout, all four passed in a separate Linux run without relaxing the 1 px limit. Candidate baselines were generated separately and visually reviewed before adoption. Build, lint, 26 color utility tests, and packed ESM imports also passed.

Route screenshots alone do not prove overlay visibility: the existing Popover screenshot does not open its overlay, the Select Popover route can fall back to Alert without an event, and the Sheet route screenshot can omit the sheet. Six additional light/dark tests now open Popover, Select Popover, and Sheet through real clicks and assert that the correct overlay content is visible and inside the viewport. These do not replace native visual acceptance of each overlay. One emulator and its palette do not establish coverage of every OEM, wallpaper, browser, or font scale. Older relative-color browser support has a guarded fallback but was not validated on an old WebView.

The supervisor and independent reviewer rechecked their returned findings after fixes. Review records and original full-resolution screenshots are retained as local review artifacts. Draft #57 remains unmerged.
